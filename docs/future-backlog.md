# After the functional pilot

Production prerequisites: verified owner recovery; approved privacy, retention and account-closure/export policy; accountable backup owner and independent backup storage; eligible hosting and persistent SQL; workload/concurrency testing; complete accessibility and real-device performance audit.

Product refinements: page histories beyond 100 entries, improve task-detail navigation/deep links, client duplicate suggestions across large databases, idempotency keys for retrying creations after an uncertain network response, clearer large-list export controls, complete automated browser regression coverage.

Only with explicit scope approval: inpatient care, billing, inventory, prescription automation, attachments/private storage, lab integrations, additional clinic locations, paid messaging, AI or account collaboration/conversion. No linked global identity or account switcher is planned.

Marketing after functional delivery: approved product screenshots, verified case studies if available, clearer launch documentation. No invented claims, analytics subscriptions or campaigns added to V1.
